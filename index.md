Privacy Policy for Ember

Effective Date: July 27, 2026

Introduction

Ember (“we”, “our”, or “the App”) respects your privacy. Ember is an
offline application designed to keep your personal information on your
device.

Information We Collect

Ember does not require you to create an account or sign in.

Ember does not collect, transmit, upload, or share your personal
information with us or any third-party servers.

Local Storage

All information you create in Ember, including: - Journal entries -
Tasks and to-do lists - Habits - Goals and progress - App preferences
and settings

is stored locally on your device only.

We do not have access to this information.

Internet and Data Sharing

Ember is designed to function fully offline.

Your data is not uploaded to our servers and is not shared with third
parties.

Advertising

Ember does not display third-party advertisements.

Children’s Privacy

Ember is not specifically directed toward children under 13. We do not
knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

Because your information remains on your device, you are responsible for
protecting access to your device. While we strive to build secure
software, no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be
completely secure.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be
reflected by changing the Effective Date.
